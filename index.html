Methodology: Merging Sentiment with Trades

1. Data Preprocessing: Parsed intraday trade execution timestamps from the Hyperliquid raw data, standardizing formats and extracting the trade_date. Casted PnL, Volume, and Fees to numeric types while removing corrupted or incomplete records.

2. Date Alignment Logic: Mapped the daily Bitcoin Fear & Greed (F&G) index values to the precise intraday trade timestamps using a left-join on trade_date. This creates a unified time-series dataset that aligns every single trade with the broader market regime of that day.

3. Handling Missing Values: Handled unclassified trades by filtering out non-matched dates. Applied forward-filling (ffill) logic for missing sentiment indices over weekends or API downtime to ensure robust, continuous aggregations without breaking the time-series model.

4. Sentiment Classification Mapping: Categorized quantitative F&G values into 5 distinct buckets for categorical analysis: Extreme Fear (0-25), Fear (26-45), Neutral (46-55), Greed (56-75), and Extreme Greed (76-100). This bucketing logic converts a continuous variable into actionable trading regimes.

5. Aggregation Logic: Grouped the merged dataset by these classification buckets to compute aggregate metrics such as Total PnL, Win Rate (profitable trades / total trades), and Volume. Segmented directional performance (Long/Buy vs Short/Sell) across regimes to identify regime-specific alpha.
